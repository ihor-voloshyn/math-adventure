# Kitten Knight — Character Master Spec v1.0

**Role:** playable Knight  
**Animal:** kitten  
**Visual status:** canonical MVP character

## Identity

A friendly young kitten with a brave fantasy-adventure identity.

## Proportions

- oversized head;
- compact heroic body;
- large expressive eyes;
- same overall visual system as the puppy characters;
- kitten-specific facial and ear silhouette.

## Class silhouette

Knight identity:
- light stylized armor;
- small sword;
- round shield.

Armor should complement the kitten silhouette rather than hide its animal identity.

## Materials

Primary material families:
- stylized metal;
- leather;
- cloth;
- fur;
- wood details.

## Equipment slots

Initial:
- base armor;
- sword;
- shield.

Future:
- helmet;
- armor variants;
- gloves;
- boots;
- alternate swords;
- alternate shields;
- accessories.

## Animation

Required MVP animations:
- idle;
- walk;
- run;
- sword attack;
- victory;
- damage reaction.

## VFX

Use restrained physical/defensive effects:
- impact flash;
- shield block;
- controlled sparks/energy.

## Modeling requirement

The kitten body must remain reusable across Kitten Mage and future growth stages.
