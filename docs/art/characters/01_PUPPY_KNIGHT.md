# Puppy Knight — Character Master Spec v1.0

**Role:** playable Knight  
**Animal:** puppy  
**Visual status:** canonical MVP character

## Identity

A friendly young puppy with an adventurous heroic personality. The character must feel capable and brave while remaining approachable for children.

## Proportions

- oversized head;
- compact heroic body;
- large expressive eyes;
- short, readable limbs;
- stable silhouette;
- same base proportions as Puppy Mage.

## Class silhouette

Knight identity must be immediately visible through:
- light stylized armor;
- small sword;
- round shield.

Do not rely only on color to communicate the class.

## Materials

Primary material families:
- stylized metal;
- leather;
- cloth;
- fur;
- wood for weapon details.

Keep surfaces polished but simplified enough for mobile.

## Equipment slots

Initial:
- base armor;
- sword;
- shield.

Future:
- helmet;
- chest armor variants;
- gloves;
- boots;
- alternate swords;
- alternate shields;
- decorative accessories.

## Animation

Required MVP animations:
- idle;
- walk;
- run;
- sword attack;
- victory;
- damage reaction.

## VFX

Knight effects should be physical/defensive:
- short impact flash;
- shield block effect;
- controlled spark/energy effect.

Avoid excessive particles.

## Modeling requirement

The model must be suitable for modular equipment and future growth stages. The base body should not need to be redesigned for every armor variant.
