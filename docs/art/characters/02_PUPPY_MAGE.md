# Puppy Mage — Character Master Spec v1.0

**Role:** playable Mage  
**Animal:** puppy  
**Visual status:** canonical MVP character

## Identity

The same puppy identity and base proportions as Puppy Knight, expressed through a magical class.

## Proportions

- oversized head;
- compact body;
- large expressive eyes;
- same base body construction as Puppy Knight;
- readable silhouette at mobile scale.

## Class silhouette

Mage identity must be immediately visible through:
- fantasy robe;
- wooden staff;
- small crystal;
- subtle magical glow.

The class must remain recognizable even when the weapon is temporarily hidden.

## Materials

Primary material families:
- cloth;
- fur;
- wood;
- crystal;
- small metal accents.

## Equipment slots

Initial:
- robe;
- staff;
- crystal/amulet.

Future:
- hats;
- robe variants;
- alternate staffs;
- amulets;
- magical accessories.

## Animation

Required MVP animations:
- idle;
- walk;
- run;
- basic staff/spell attack;
- victory;
- damage reaction.

## VFX

Mage effects should communicate magic:
- soft glow;
- small particles;
- crystal emission;
- controlled spell trail.

Effects must remain readable without obscuring the character.

## Modeling requirement

The base body must remain compatible with the Puppy Knight body system so animal identity is preserved across classes.
