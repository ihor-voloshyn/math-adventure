# Kitten Mage — Character Master Spec v1.0

**Role:** playable Mage  
**Animal:** kitten  
**Visual status:** canonical MVP character

## Identity

A friendly young kitten with a magical fantasy identity.

## Proportions

- oversized head;
- compact body;
- large expressive eyes;
- same overall visual system as the other three canonical characters;
- kitten-specific face and ears.

## Class silhouette

Mage identity:
- fantasy robe;
- wooden staff;
- small crystal;
- subtle magical particles and glow.

The class must be recognizable from silhouette and materials.

## Materials

Primary material families:
- cloth;
- fur;
- wood;
- crystal;
- small metal accents.

## Equipment slots

Initial:
- robe;
- staff;
- crystal/amulet.

Future:
- hats;
- robe variants;
- alternate staffs;
- amulets;
- magical accessories.

## Animation

Required MVP animations:
- idle;
- walk;
- run;
- staff/spell attack;
- victory;
- damage reaction.

## VFX

Use:
- soft magical glow;
- crystal emission;
- small particles;
- controlled spell trail.

Do not allow VFX to hide the character or reduce task readability.

## Modeling requirement

The kitten body must be reusable across Kitten Knight and future growth stages.
