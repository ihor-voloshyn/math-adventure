## Confirmed Hero concept
- Hero = the character itself: kitten or puppy.
- Start choice = Cat or Dog.
- No gender selection.
- Cat/Dog is not a separate pet.
- Hero grows visually: kitten/puppy → young → adult.
- Hero can develop toward Knight or Mage.
- Equipment is worn directly by the Hero and affects both appearance and gameplay stats.
- Mastery is independent of RPG progression and gameplay bonuses.

## Confirmed companion Pet concept
- Separate pets can accompany the Hero.
- Initial concept list: rabbit, fox, dragon, owl, squirrel, turtle, hedgehog, frog.
- Only a small subset should appear in MVP; more can unlock later.
- Pets can visually develop and can provide gameplay bonuses (Attack, Defense, Hearts, Ability Power and other Combat Modifiers).
- Pet bonuses do not modify Mastery, Adaptive Engine decisions, or mathematical correctness.
- Final roster remains extensible.
