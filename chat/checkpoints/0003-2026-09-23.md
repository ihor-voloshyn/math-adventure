## Architecture state

- Math Engine remains authoritative for mathematical truth.
- Adaptive Engine chooses learning target/mode/difficulty/context and does not own rewards.
- Task Generator creates concrete tasks and does not own correctness or Mastery.
- Game Progression owns XP/Coins/Loot rewards.
- Inventory/Equipment owns item ownership and equipped state.
- Combat consumes resolved combat stats and does not mutate Inventory/Equipment.
- Equipment visual state and gameplay state are derived from persisted Equipment + Inventory state.
- Android SharedPreferences persistence remains a prototype boundary, not the future server-of-truth.
